## Why

The CV, this site and the owner's job-search profile were edited by hand in three places and drifted apart (a "Senior" title, an internal call volume, internal system names). The owner now keeps a single source of career facts with visibility levels and generates every destination from it with his `carrera` skill. The site should read the generated content instead of keeping its own copy, so an update means editing one file and regenerating.

## What Changes

- Add `src/data/carrera.json`, generated by the `carrera` skill (not edited by hand): years per technology, stack groups, private-project ids and tech, and per-language experience, leadership, project descriptions and education.
- `Hero`, `Experience`, `Stack`, `Projects` (private projects), `Leadership` and `Education` read `carrera.json` through a small `src/data/carrera.js` helper that picks the active language.
- Remove the now-duplicated prose from `en.json`/`es.json` (experience, leadership, education items and private-project descriptions) and the matching arrays from `profile.js`; keep section titles and UI strings.
- Content from the source: Angular added to the years block (2); new "Architecture & integration" stack group; Pyansa bullets ordered integration-first; WMS described as front and back end; no "230 tests" on the media card; VLM bootcamp in Education; Java-only QA tools dropped from Stack.
- About paragraph 1 states the owner's focus on integrations, deployments and architecture.
