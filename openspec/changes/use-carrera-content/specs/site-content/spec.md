## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Approved figures only
Years of experience SHALL be limited to Python 3, Angular 2, React 1 and TypeScript 1. The site MUST NOT contain "5+ years" or any larger figure for software experience.

#### Scenario: No inflated claims
- **WHEN** the text and translations are searched for "5+" years claims
- **THEN** none are found

### Requirement: Bilingual text
All visible prose SHALL exist in Spanish and English with identical keys, both in `es.json`/`en.json` and in the `es`/`en` blocks of `src/data/carrera.json`.

#### Scenario: Key parity
- **WHEN** the keys of `es.json` and `en.json`, and of the `es` and `en` blocks of `carrera.json`, are compared
- **THEN** the sets are equal

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Generated career content
Years, stack, experience, leadership, private-project descriptions and education SHALL come from `src/data/carrera.json`, generated by the owner's `carrera` skill from his single source of career facts; that file MUST NOT be edited by hand.

#### Scenario: Update a fact
- **WHEN** the owner changes a fact in his career source and regenerates
- **THEN** the site shows it after rebuilding, with no edits to components or translation files
